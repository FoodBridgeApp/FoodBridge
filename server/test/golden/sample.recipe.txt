Spaghetti Aglio e Olio
Ingredients:
- 200 g spaghetti
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic (thinly sliced)
- 1 tsp chili flakes
- salt
- parsley (optional)

Instructions:
1. Cook spaghetti in salted boiling water until al dente.
2. Warm olive oil, gently fry garlic and chili.
3. Toss pasta with oil mixture; add a splash of pasta water.
4. Season, garnish with parsley, serve hot.
